Joseph Vijay Chandrasekhar (born 22 June 1974), known as Vijay, is a leading Indian actor and politician in Tamil cinema. With a career spanning over three decades and nearly 70 films, he is one of the highest-paid actors, often referred to as "Thalapathy". He launched his political party, Tamilaga Vettri Kazhagam, in February 2024. Here are key details about actor Vijay: Age: As of June 2025, Vijay is 51 years old (born 22 June 1974). Early Career: Born in Madras to film director S.A. Chandrasekhar, he debuted as a child actor in Vetri (1984) and as a lead actor in Naalaiya Theerpu (1992) at age 18. Film Stardom: Known for hits like Ghilli, Pokkiri, Thuppakki, Mersal, and Leo, he has a massive fan following and is a major commercial force in Tamil cinema. Politics: In February 2024, he entered politics by founding the Tamilaga Vettri Kazhagam (TVK) party, transitioning from his film career. Personal Life: He is married to Sangeeta (m. 1999) and has two children. Other Prominent Personalities Named Vijay: Vijay Sethupathi: Prolific Tamil actor known for Master and Maharaja. Vijay Deverakonda: Telugu actor known for Arjun Reddy. Vijay Antony: Tamil music composer and actor (Pichaikkaran). Duniya Vijay: Kannada actor and director. Vijay Varma: Hindi film/OTT actor known for Dahaad and Darlings.
